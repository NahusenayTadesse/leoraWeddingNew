Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

1. What We Collect

CategoryExamples
Account infoName, email, phone number, password
Wedding detailsDate, guest count, budget, saved vendors

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate core features like matching couples with vendors and processing bookings
  • To send booking confirmations, messages, and account notifications
  • To improve search relevance and platform performance
  • To detect fraud and enforce our Terms of Service

3. What We Share

When you send a booking request, we share relevant wedding details (date, guest count, budget range) with that vendor. We do not sell personal data to third parties. We may share data with payment processors, cloud hosting providers, and analytics tools strictly to operate the service.

4. Data Retention

We retain account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required for legal or accounting purposes.

5. Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or export your personal data from Account Settings
  • Request account deletion at any time
  • Opt out of marketing emails while still receiving essential account notifications

6. Cookies

We use cookies to keep you logged in, remember preferences like light/dark mode, and understand how the platform is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

7. Security

We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest, and payment details are handled by PCI-compliant payment processors — Leora Events does not store full card numbers.

8. Children's Privacy

Leora Events is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data requests, reach out via our Contact page.
