Why we started Leora

Wedding planning in Ethiopia has always relied on word of mouth, endless phone calls, and spreadsheets passed between family members. We started Leora Events to bring that same trusted, community feel online — with real vendor availability, transparent pricing, and tools that keep budgets and guest lists organized in one place.

What we stand for

Transparency first. Vendor pricing, availability, and reviews are visible upfront — no hidden fees or surprise quotes.

Built for Ethiopia. From Telebirr payments to multi-day celebration planning, every feature is designed around how weddings actually happen here.

A platform vendors can grow on. Small businesses get the same visibility tools as larger ones, with fair, flexible pricing plans.
